## Installation & Setup

### Q5: Is installation free?
**A:** Yes! We provide completely free installation at your location. No hidden charges.

### Q6: How long does installation take?
**A:** Typical installation takes 2-4 hours depending on space requirements and electrical setup.

### Q7: What are the space requirements?
**A:** Machines require:
- Minimum floor space: 2.5 ft x 2 ft
- Height clearance: 6 ft minimum
- Stable, even flooring
- Nearby power outlet (standard 15A)
- Safe, accessible location

### Q8: Do you provide power connections?
**A:** We don't install new electrical connections, but we work with your electrician if needed. We require a standard 15A power outlet within 10 feet of the machine location.

### Q9: What if my space doesn't have an outlet?
**A:** We can recommend licensed electricians to install one. Electrical work costs are borne by you.

### Q10: How long is the installation process from booking to operational?
**A:** Typically 7-14 days after confirming your location and signing the service agreement.

## Troubleshooting

### Q38: The machine is not turning on. What should I do?
**A:** Check:
1. Power outlet is working
2. Machine power switch is ON
3. No tripped circuit breakers
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Q39: The screen is frozen or unresponsive.
**A:** Try:
1. Wait 30 seconds
2. Press the reset button (if visible)
3. Power cycle: Turn off for 2 minutes, then on
4. Contact support if unresolved

### Q40: How do I report a jammed product?
**A:** 
1. Don't force the machine
2. Contact support immediately
3. Provide item code, location, and photo
4. We'll arrange resolution (refund/replacement)

### Q41: The card reader isn't working.
**A:**
1. Try another payment method
2. Report to support
3. We'll repair or replace the reader
4. Card payment will be restored

### Q42: What if I lost my receipt?

- Digital receipts available via email
- Contact support with transaction details
- Provide time and location
- We can verify and assist
